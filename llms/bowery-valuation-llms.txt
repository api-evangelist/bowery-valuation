## What Bowery does

- Commercial real estate appraisal for banks, agency (Fannie Mae / Freddie Mac) lenders, funds and
  private lenders.
- Asset types: multifamily, mixed-use, office, retail, industrial and warehouse, self-storage,
  condo and co-op, affordable housing, land and development sites, ground-up construction.
- Coverage: all 50 U.S. states, with offices concentrated in NY, CA, CO, FL, GA, IL, IN, MA, NJ,
  NC, PA, WA, DC and WI.
- Compliance posture stated publicly: state-certified appraisers trained to USPAP, FIRREA and GSE
  guidelines, with compliance checks built into the platform.
